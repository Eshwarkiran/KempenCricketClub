What happens next

  • We'll reply to the email address you gave us — so keep an eye on your inbox (and just in case, your spam folder).
  • If you asked about joining, we'll help you book your free taster sessions and tell you what to bring.
  • If you registered as a member, we'll confirm your details and the next steps, including how to pay your membership fee.

In the meantime, feel free to explore the site or follow us for club news. If you need to reach us directly, email contact@kempencricket.be.

Registering your children or family too? Just register each one with the same email — we'll email you a link to approve them onto your account.
