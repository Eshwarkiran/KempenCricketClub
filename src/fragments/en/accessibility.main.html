Accessibility Statement

Cricket is for everyone, and so is our website. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it comfortably.

Last updated: 4 July 2026.

Our commitment

Kempen Cricket Club is committed to making its website accessible to everyone, including people who use assistive technology or who browse with a keyboard, screen reader, or on a small screen. We aim to meet the international Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA as a practical target.

What we do

  • We use clear, structured headings and meaningful page titles.
  • We write descriptive text for meaningful images.
  • We aim for strong colour contrast and readable text sizes.
  • The site works with a keyboard and adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.
  • We provide the site in both English and Dutch.

Known limitations

This site is still growing, and some areas are a work in progress. Embedded third-party content (such as maps) may not be fully accessible, and some older documents may not yet meet every guideline. We are improving these over time.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or you have a suggestion to make it better, please tell us — your feedback genuinely helps. Email contact@kempencricket.be and we will do our best to help and to fix the issue.

Review

We review this statement as the site develops and as we make improvements.
