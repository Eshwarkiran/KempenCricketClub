Fees

Simple, affordable, everyone welcome

One annual fee covers your season — affiliation with Cricket Vlaanderen (once confirmed), access to all training, selection for matches and friendlies, and the use of club equipment if you don't yet have your own.

A junior is anyone aged 16 or under on the first day of the season. Thomas More students and families are arranged directly with the club — your rate is confirmed when you register. Prefer to pay in instalments? Just ask — no one should miss out on cricket for financial reasons.

Good to know: most Belgian health funds (mutualiteit) reimburse part of a sports membership each year — ask us for the form after you join.

What you get

What your membership covers

Included

  • Access to all club training sessions through the season.
  • Selection for club matches and friendlies.
  • Use of club cricket equipment if you don't have your own.
  • Affiliation and registration with Cricket Vlaanderen / BCF.
  • Insurance during club activities — being arranged via Cricket Vlaanderen; at your own risk until confirmed.

Not included

  • Your own jersey, if you'd like one.
  • Travel to away sessions, matches or clubs.
Your first session

Honestly, it's friendlier than you think

Most people at your first session will be beginners too. We'll show you the basics, put a bat in your hands early, and keep it light. Wear comfortable sports kit and trainers, bring water, and arrive a few minutes early so we can say hello.

  • Bring: sports kit, trainers, water.
  • We provide: bats, balls, pads — all the gear.
  • For juniors: a parent or guardian completes sign-up, and is welcome to stay and watch.
