Why us

A genuine local story, not a logo on a billboard

Backing Kempen CC isn't a big corporate deal — it's a real, visible contribution to sport and community in the Kempen. Here's what makes this moment worth being part of:

Local goodwill

You're helping families, juniors and newcomers in Geel and the surrounding towns find a sport to call their own.

A growing community

The Kempen is home to people from across the cricketing world. A club like ours connects them — and the businesses that support it.

An Olympic sport rising

Cricket returns to the Olympic Games at Los Angeles 2028. You're backing a sport with real momentum.

What you get

Ways we can say thank you

We keep this flexible and honest — we'd rather agree something that genuinely fits you than sell a fixed package. Depending on the partnership, that can include:

  • Kit & equipment branding — your name on training gear or club equipment.
  • Session & ground banners at training and matches.
  • Web & news placement — a logo slot on our site and a mention in club news.
  • Name a junior group — lend your name to an age group or programme.

We don't publish fixed price tiers — we'll always be clear about what your support pays for and the difference it makes.
