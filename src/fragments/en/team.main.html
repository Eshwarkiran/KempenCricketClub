Advisory

Guidance from experience

MR
Senior Club Mentor & Advisor

M.V. Narasimha Rao "Bobby"

Former Indian Test cricketer and legendary international coach, renowned for his world-class technical expertise. Bobby captained Hyderabad to a historic Ranji Trophy title, spent decades shaping international cricket — famously mentoring Indian great VVS Laxman and coaching nine players of Ireland's inaugural Test squad — and was awarded an MBE for his exceptional services to the sport. At KCC he brings that elite background to sharpen our on-field performance, design advanced training structures and inspire a new generation of cricketers in our community.
