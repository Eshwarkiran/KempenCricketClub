Questions, answered

New to the club or to cricket itself? Here's everything people usually want to know. Still stuck? Just get in touch.

Getting started
Do I need any experience?
None at all. Most people at our sessions are beginners. We'll show you the basics and put a bat in your hands early — you'll be playing, not just watching.
I've never even seen a full game. Can I still come?
Absolutely — that's most of us. If you're curious, our plain-language Learn Cricket guide explains the whole game in a few minutes, and you can test yourself with Gully. But you don't need any of that to turn up and have a go.
Do I need my own equipment?
No. We provide bats, balls and pads. Just bring comfortable sports kit, trainers and water. A jersey of your own is optional, later.
How do I try it before joining?
Book three free tryout sessions through the Join page. No cost, no commitment — come along, see how you like it, and decide afterwards.
Who can join
What ages do you take?
Juniors from age 6 through 16, and adults 17 and over. We started with juniors, but the club is open to all ages.
My child is 10 — can they join?
Yes. Juniors from age 6 are welcome. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian completes the registration and signs on their behalf.
Can women and girls play?
Yes. Cricket at KCC is open to everyone, of any gender. If you'd like to know about taster sessions, get in touch.
Cost & membership
What does membership cost?
Adults €70 per season, juniors (16 and under) €50, and non-playing supporting members €25. One fee covers training, match selection, club equipment and federation affiliation.
Are there discounts?
Thomas More students (16+) and families of three or more from the same household have special arrangements — get in touch and we'll confirm your rate.
Can I get part of the fee back?
Often, yes. Most Belgian health funds (mutualiteit) reimburse part of a sports membership each year — bring us the form. If you receive OCMW support, they may help too.
Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. If paying in one go is difficult, just ask and we'll arrange a personal payment plan. No one should miss out on cricket for financial reasons.
What if I leave part-way through the season?
You can resign at any time, but membership fees already paid aren't refunded — that's why we offer three free tryout sessions first, so you can be sure before you join.
Sessions & practicalities
When and where do you train?
We train in and around Geel, at local indoor and outdoor facilities. Current session times are shared when you register — book a tryout and we'll send you the details.
Which towns do you serve?
We're based in Geel and welcome players from across the Kempen — Mol, Balen, Westerlo, Olen, Kasterlee, Herentals, Laakdal, Meerhout and Tessenderlo among them.
What language are sessions in?
A friendly mix. Sessions run in a welcoming blend of Dutch and English, and we're actively growing our Dutch-speaking coaching. Cricket's own vocabulary stays in English everywhere.
About the club
Are you affiliated with a federation?
We're in conversation with Cricket Vlaanderen and the Belgian Cricket Federation about affiliation. We'll say so plainly the moment it's official — until then, it's "working toward."
Is there insurance?
Insurance cover during club activities is being arranged through Cricket Vlaanderen affiliation. Until that's confirmed, participation is at your own risk — we'll update this the moment it's in place.
How is my data handled?
We only collect what we need to run membership and federation registration, and never sell or share it beyond Cricket Vlaanderen / BCF for affiliation. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data anytime via contact@kempencricket.be.

Still have a question? Email us at contact@kempencricket.be — a real person will get back to you.
