Cookie Policy

The small files our website uses, why we use them, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 4 July 2026.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They help the site work, remember your choices, and — only if you allow it — help us understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

The cookies we use

Strictly necessary (always on)

These are needed for the site to function and cannot be switched off. They include remembering your cookie choice and, in the member area, keeping you securely signed in. They do not track you.

Functional (always on, no tracking)

These remember preferences such as your chosen language (English or Dutch) so you don't have to select it every time.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you allow it, we use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are helpful. This data is aggregated and is never used to identify you or sold to anyone. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set.

We do not use advertising cookies

Kempen Cricket Club does not run ads and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Managing your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time using the button below, or by clearing cookies in your browser settings.

More information

For how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? Email contact@kempencricket.be.
